3. Smart Money Market Structure (SMC)

TradeLine AI doesn't rely on lagging moving averages to determine the trend. Instead, it uses pure price action and Smart Money Concepts (SMC) to identify the true market trend in real-time by tracking swing highs, swing lows, and institutional imbalances.

  • FVG (Fair Value Gaps): Also known as imbalances. When price moves so aggressively that buyers and sellers are unable to fairly transact, it leaves a gap (highlighted as green or red boxes on the chart). These zones act as highly probable magnetic targets and support/resistance zones for future price action.
  • BOS (Break of Structure): This indicates trend continuation. In an uptrend, a BOS occurs when a candle closes above the most recent swing high. In a downtrend, it happens when price closes below the most recent swing low.
  • CHoCH (Change of Character): This signals a trend reversal. If we are in an uptrend and the price suddenly drops and closes below the most recent swing low, a Bearish CHoCH is triggered, instantly flipping the system's trend detection to a Downtrend.
  • AI Analysis Integration: All live SMC data, including FVGs and Order Blocks, are fed directly into the Gemini AI Engine to provide institutional-grade trading context.

4. Automated 3+ Bounce S/R Detection

Finding accurate Support and Resistance (S/R) levels is the foundation of technical analysis. Our system uses a proprietary algorithm to scan historical price data and automatically plot highly accurate S/R lines.

  • Strict Validation: Levels are only drawn if they have withstood at least 3 distinct price bounces.
  • Strength Scoring: S/R lines are visually weighted; thicker lines indicate stronger historical significance.
  • Click-to-Focus: Clicking any S/R level in the analytics panel instantly snaps your chart to that exact price point.
