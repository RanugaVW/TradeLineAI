TradeLine AI Documentation

Welcome to the official documentation for TradeLine AI. Learn how our Agentic AI and automated analysis tools can elevate your cryptocurrency trading strategy.

1. Gemini Agentic AI Advisor

TradeLine AI features a powerful Agentic AI system built on top of Gemini. The AI doesn't just read data; it actively analyzes your current chart viewport, assesses your LKR to USD budget, and generates actionable, context-aware trading signals (Buy, Sell, Hold).

User Action Required: Rename your AI screenshot to ai-agent.png and place it in the same folder as this file, or update this src path.

  • Context Aware: The agent analyzes the exact candlesticks currently visible on your screen.
  • Budget Optimization: Input your LKR budget, and the AI calculates your position sizing in real-time based on live Binance conversion rates.
  • Risk Analysis: Provides detailed breakdowns of trade risk and potential R/R ratio.

2. Automated 3+ Bounce S/R Detection

Finding accurate Support and Resistance (S/R) levels is the foundation of technical analysis. Our system uses a proprietary algorithm to scan historical price data and automatically plot highly accurate S/R lines.

User Action Required: Rename your S/R detection screenshot to sr-detection.png and place it in the same folder as this file, or update this src path.

  • Strict Validation: Levels are only drawn if they have withstood at least 3 distinct price bounces.
  • Strength Scoring: S/R lines are visually weighted; thicker lines indicate stronger historical significance.
  • Click-to-Focus: Clicking any S/R level in the analytics panel instantly snaps your chart to that exact price point.
